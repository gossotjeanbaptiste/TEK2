🚀 DÉMARRAGE RAPIDE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1️⃣  Vérification pré-déploiement:
   bash ansible-deployment/checklist.sh

2️⃣  Validation de syntaxe:
   cd ansible-deployment
   ansible-playbook playbook.yml --syntax-check

3️⃣  Aperçu des changements (dry-run):
   ansible-playbook -i ../my-inventory playbook.yml --check -v

4️⃣  Déploiement:
   bash deploy.sh
   
   OU
   
   ansible-playbook -i ../my-inventory playbook.yml -v
